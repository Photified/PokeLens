How to use

  1. Tap Scan a card and allow the camera.
  2. Fill the frame with one upright card. Keep it flat and avoid glare.
  3. Hold steady. Possible matches and prices appear automatically. Scroll to compare, then tap your card.

You can also scan a clear photo. No card names or numbers to type.

Prices & coverage

TCGplayer market prices in US dollars, supplied by TCGCSV. Market price is a reference, not a condition-specific offer or a graded-card valuation.

All available numbered singles from the Pokémon catalog are included. English text recognition is supported. Cards absent from the provider, unreadable scans, foreign-language cards, and certain promos may not be recognized.

Foil, stamps and edition differences cannot always be distinguished from one photo. When several printings match, each candidate shows its available market prices. Missing prices are never shown as $0.

Prices refresh when the GitHub data workflow runs. Check the date on your result. Photos stay on this device; listing images are loaded from TCGplayer.
